• Zone A → Zone B, Zone C  |  • Zone B → Zone A, Zone C, Zone D  |  • Zone C → Zone A, Zone B, Zone D  |  • Zone D → Zone B, Zone C, Zone E  |  • Zone E → Zone C, Zone D
